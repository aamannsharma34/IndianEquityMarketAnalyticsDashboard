Building turnover history — the first run of the day pulls data for ~200 stocks and can take 1–2 minutes. After that it's cached and instant.
What this can and cannot do. It measures where money is concentrating right now and tests whether that has historically carried into the next session on your own data. It is not a forecast. Most of what drives tomorrow's flow — earnings, RBI policy, global moves overnight, a single block deal — isn't in this dataset at all. Treat a high relative reading as "something is happening here, go look," not as a signal to buy. Check the measured edge above before relying on the persistence logic: if it's near zero, the ranking has no predictive content and only describes today.
