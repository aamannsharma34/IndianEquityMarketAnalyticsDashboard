What options think the close is

How the synthetic is built. For each strike near the money, synthetic = call − put + strike. With European index options that recovers the market's implied forward level. Bid-ask midpoints are used in preference to last traded price, because an LTP on a quiet strike can be minutes stale. Strikes are weighted by spread tightness, traded volume and closeness to the money.

Why it differs from spot legitimately. The synthetic is a forward, so it sits above or below spot by the cost of carry, and the two converge at expiry. A persistent gap during normal hours is carry, not a signal. It becomes informative in the CAS window, when spot is frozen and the synthetic keeps moving.

What is not here. NSE's official indicative equilibrium price during the auction is a specialised exchange feed and is not available through Kite. During the window it is published alongside quotes on the NSE website. The synthetic below is an independent, options-derived estimate — related, but not the same number.

Dispersion is your reliability check. If parity held perfectly, every strike would give the same synthetic. Wide dispersion across strikes means stale or wide quotes, and the consensus should be trusted less. It is flagged when that happens.
